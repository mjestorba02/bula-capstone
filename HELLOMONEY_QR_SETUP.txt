BESTLINK SMART PAYMENT PORTAL
HelloMoney / QR Ph Payment Flow

WHAT WAS ADDED
1. Student selects a fee and amount.
2. Selecting "Hello Money App (HMA)" opens a QR Ph checkout section.
3. The system creates a unique payment reference and a 30-minute payment record.
4. LIVE MODE:
   - Set PAYMONGO_SECRET_KEY and PAYMONGO_PUBLIC_KEY.
   - Set HELLOMONEY_MODE=live.
   - The backend creates a QR Ph Payment Intent and displays the returned QR.
   - The student scans the QR using HelloMoney (HelloMoney supports QR Ph scanning).
   - The portal polls the provider and marks the payment Paid after the provider reports success.
   - An electronic receipt is generated and stored in backend/uploads/payment_receipts.
5. DEMO MODE (default):
   - No real money is collected.
   - A demo QR is shown so the capstone flow can be demonstrated safely.
   - Use "Simulate HelloMoney Confirmation (Demo)" to demonstrate the automatic status/receipt part.
   - This demo QR must NOT be presented as a real merchant QR.

IMPORTANT FOR LIVE DEPLOYMENT
HelloMoney is the payer app. For real school collection, the school needs a merchant-side QR Ph/acquirer/payment-provider arrangement and valid credentials. The system is prepared for a QR Ph provider flow; do not put secret keys in frontend JavaScript.

ENVIRONMENT
HELLOMONEY_MODE=demo
PAYMONGO_SECRET_KEY=
PAYMONGO_PUBLIC_KEY=

For XAMPP, configure these environment variables in Apache/PHP or use your hosting environment settings.
Do not commit live secret keys into the ZIP.
